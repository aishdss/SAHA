## About

SAHA is an all-in-one transport assistant that brings every mode of city transport — BMTC buses, BMRCL metro, autos, taxis, FASTag, airport, railway stations, and the Majestic bus platform — into a single platform. Instead of navigating multiple websites and apps, or figuring out which authority to contact for a problem, users get routes, live timings, and AI-powered support in one place.

Built for tourists, newcomers, temporary settlers, students and working professionals from outside the city, and everyday commuters who want a simpler way to move around and get help when something goes wrong.

## Why SAHA

- **All in one place** — no more switching between BMTC, BMRCL, and other separate apps/sites for basic transport info.
- **You don't need to know who to contact** — SAHA figures out the right authority and routes your issue there for you.
- **Support that actually follows through** — from AI first-response to human handoff, every complaint is tracked until it's resolved.

## Core Features

### Transport Information
- Live timings for buses and trains
- Routes, stops, and timetables across all modes
- Multi-modal journey planning (metro + bus + auto/walk combined)
- Fare calculator per mode

### Search. Ask. Report. Track. Resolve.
A 3-layer AI-driven support system:

1. **AI First Response** — understands the query, answers directly if it's informational, or extracts structured details if it's a genuine complaint.
2. **Analysis & Routing** — classifies urgency (safety > operational > informational), routes the issue to the correct authority, merges duplicate reports from the same location/route, and keeps the user updated on status.
3. **Human Handoff** — when the AI can't resolve it, urgency is high, or the user is unsatisfied, a human agent takes over with full context already provided — no repeating yourself.

### Authority Dashboard
- Filtered complaint queue (noise-free — only genuine issues, not routine questions)
- Recurring problem detection and predictive analytics
- Resolution SLA tracking
- Verified-authority badges so users know responses are genuine

### Community & Reliability
- Crowdsourced micro-reporting (crowding, safety issues, broken infrastructure)
- Offline/low-connectivity fallback with cached data
- Multilingual support (English + Kannada)

## User Roles
- **User** — search transport info, ask questions, report issues, track resolution
- **Authority** — manage complaint queues, view analytics, respond to verified issues

## Target Audience
Tourists, new city residents, temporary settlers, students and IT professionals relocating from other cities, and general commuters.
